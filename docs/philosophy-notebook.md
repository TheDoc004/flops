# FLOPS as a notebook

FLOPS does not nag, auto-advance your day, or invent a life for you. It is a **notebook**:
you write ingredients, meals, lifts, and supplements; AI adds insight when you ask
(speak-to-log, estimates, summaries).

## Principles

1. **You write; the app holds the page.** The calendar day you are viewing stays put until
   **you** flip it. Midnight does not silently jump you into tomorrow while you still need
   to check off a nighttime supplement.
2. **AI is opt-in insight.** Speak or type a meal when that is faster. Logging a saved recipe
   or library ingredient never requires an AI call.
3. **Coach view is read + summarize.** Coaches see what clients already logged. They do not
   rewrite the client’s notebook in v1.
4. **No busywork.** Prefer dense lists, one clear entry path per job (Log Meal = receipt;
   AI Estimate = speak/describe), and quiet chrome.
